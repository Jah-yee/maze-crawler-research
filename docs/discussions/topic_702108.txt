TL;DR

Pure rule-based agent, ~370 lines of Python. No ML, no MCTS, no neural nets. The core insight: this game is entirely about factory pathfinding speed.

Key Findings from Replay Analysis

I analyzed 29 replays of top players and found:



Zero top players build workers, miners, or do any mining

Winners move their factory every single turn (0 IDLE)

The only unit worth building is 1 scout for cheap vision

Factory gets 30-55 SOUTH moves and 8-17 jumps per 500-step game


The 3 Techniques That Matter

1. Jump-Preferred BFS

Factory jumps cover 2 cells in 1 turn. Normal walking = 1 cell per 2 turns (move_period=2). So a jump is ~4x more efficient. My BFS explores jump moves before walk moves when the cooldown is ready, making the factory actively seek paths that use jumps.

2. Mirror Vision

The maze has E/W symmetry. Every wall I observe on column c, I can infer the mirrored wall on column width-1-c. This doubles explored area instantly and makes BFS much more accurate through fog.

3. Optimistic Fog Treatment

Unknown cells are treated as passable (wall bits = 0). This means BFS will route through fog optimistically rather than being scared of unexplored areas. Combined with the scout running ahead, this works remarkably well.
